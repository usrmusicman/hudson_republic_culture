# Summary

## Story
The set lights come back into focus with a cute baby beaver in a scouts uniform and blue handkerchief (not to be confused with the standard red one) going down the path with "We Are The Hudson" sign on it.  
It sees the Maple Leaf falling off a Maple tree and skips on by.  
Baby beaver explores past the train tracks, across the river for new friends and adventures.  
A female soprano soloist narrates the scene, via the lyrics being singing.  

## Influences
This is the scene where the Hudson Republic is born.  

## Easter Eggs
From St. Thomas to the 'peg, we will not beg.: This is the reference to what was once Manitoba and Ontario. Now it is the Hudson Republic territory.  
We are no Maple Leaf, we are sovereign beings.: This shows the new nation as active participant beavers, instead of passive Maple Leaf crown worshipers.  
Long live the Hudson!: The establishment of the new Republic.
