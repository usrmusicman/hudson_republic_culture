# Summary

## Story
Our father character goes to the trucker convoy of February 2022, after a vaccine injury happened to his wife to protest Ottawa.  

## Influences
This is the Magus Opus, turning point in the musical where the people see the government for what it is.  
A family friendly event, ripped apart by Ottawa.  
Government overreach and propaganda.  

## Easter Eggs
Where driving big red takes some skill!: A mention to the signature truck of the protest called "Big Red".  
They let freeloaders raid the paper route!: This is a mention about mass-immigration and them getting all the benefits instead.  
Alberta just wants to sell black gold!...: This is a Beverly hillbillies reference (Texas Tee, Black Gold).  
They are worse than my children who raid the Loonie bin...: References Canada as a Loony bin and Ottawa raiding people's personal accounts.  
Why be a politician when you're caught stealing from the cabinet?...: This is a metaphor when a child steals and when politicians steal tax payer dollars.  
I don't want to wear a diaper on my face!... Why is Ottawa leading a society it tries to erase?...: This is about the face masks causing people health issues with pro-longed use. Also, the great replacement theory.
