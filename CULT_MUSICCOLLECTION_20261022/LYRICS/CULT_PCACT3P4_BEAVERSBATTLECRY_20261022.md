# Summary

## Story
Brad rushes Peggy to safety before the red coats can capture her.  
Brad then takes on the corrupt RCMP, politicians, leftists, UN Blue helmets, all of the enemies of true sovereignty.  
Brad takes them on his resurrected ghost elephant pet Jumbo in one final battle.  
Brad gets power ups and goes "Beaver Saiyan" and his ultra-powered up takes on the last of the establishment and defeats his enemies with the hyper-tail slap.  
Then his pet elephant Jumbo stomps its foot on the legacy Maple Leaf, while Brad the beaver raises the "Hudson Republic" flag.  
Act 3 ends slowly cutting to black on set.  

## Influences
This is the final stand against the establishment Ottawa.

The Maple has lost its crown!: The death of the crown and the monarchy bondage.

Jumbo is making his tour, while Ottawa tries political parkour!: Jumbo is going straight for the establishment as they twist tie themselves around the wooden posts, Jumbo crushes them.  
Slap that tail!: Is the rest of the beaver backup coming in to clean up the joint and to clean Ottawa's clock.
