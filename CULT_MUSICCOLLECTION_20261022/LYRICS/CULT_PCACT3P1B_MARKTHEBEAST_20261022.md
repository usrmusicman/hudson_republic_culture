# Summary

## Story
Mark Carney pulls the blindfold off and unties the daughter.  
She sees a bunch of hooded WEF cultists chanting welcome to Carneyville.  
She then sees an inverted pentagram with her name "Peggy" in red wax on it.  
She panics as she is placed in the middle of the circle with the lit candles.  

## Influences
This scene is inspired from "Silence of the Lambs", "Saw" and the "The Shining"  

## Easter Eggs
I bask in the sun, drinking their redrum...: Redrum is murder backwards and this is from The Shining.  
Look who's here to play. Are you ready to play a game?: This line is from the original Saw trilogy.  
Are you ready for the Deed of Surrender to happen again?: This is mentioning the 2026 EU Deed of Surrender currently going on and eerily bringing up parallels to the 1866-1870 period of Canada's history.  
I am your commander and cheat on Parliament Hill...: A tongue-in-cheek play on the US President's Commander and Chief position within the US republic.
