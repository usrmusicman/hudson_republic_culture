# Summary

## Story
The Donald (Donald Trump) is stepping on a Maple Leaf mat crushing it firmly under his foot.  
He is cool and calm at the podium giving his speech about the mooching Maple Leaf.  
Trump doesn't like globalism and wants nothing to do with the Maple Leaf.  
He compares Canada to a Las Vegas prostitute and he sees himself as the brass in the relationship.  

## Influences
The redpill community and its tropes were heavily influential in this piece.  
The musical inspiration theme for this piece is "Willy Wanka and the Chocolate Factory"  

## Easter Eggs
The seventh seal! Art of the deal!: Revelations 8 reference.  
Just like the red pill rejects Pinterest!: A jab at Pintrest (they personally banned my account for political reasons).  
People call him Augustus Gloop: This is a reference to an October 2016 CNN political panel comment.  
It's not a Labatt, Mark calls him a rat.: This is a reference to the 2026 American liquor in Canada.
