# Summary

## Story
A father is spending his last week in the City of Toronto before he leaves the city with his family for good.  
He has found a remote position and can't wait to escape what he calls the "Metropolis Knife".  

## Influences
Escape from New York themed.  
It makes fun of the "free the weed" movement of the early 2010s.  

## Easter Eggs
Even the thieves can't escape with a bag of gold!: Referencing the major gold heist in Toronto Pearson International of 2023.  
The barricade once stood until a maniac's reign, now it's just players running wild in the GTA game!: This is a direct reference to Alek Minassian's Hollywood style vehicle mass-murder event.  
The sky-condoms to the Great Lakes were never that clean: It compares the Toronto skyline to a safe-sex device, especially the CN-Tower (That is the sky condom). Its also a play on the word condominiums (they are disposable).  
How can anyone come here and pay six-K rent?: This is based on a CBC Marketplace 2022 episode where a lady is spending 9K a month on a "new condo", it could barely fit a couple, let alone a family. This episode exposed the scam known as Toronto.  
The barricade once stood until a maniac's reign: The barricade is a Les Miserables reference.  
The "National Razor" is a French Revolution term.
