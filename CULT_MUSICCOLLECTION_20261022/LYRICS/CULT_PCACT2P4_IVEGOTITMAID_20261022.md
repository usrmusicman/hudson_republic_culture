# Summary

## Story
Bob is a husband who is being sent into the hospital because he can't get a doctor to diagnosis to a rare sleeping condition.  
His wife got him to sign a life insurance policy before she sent him in for the "treatment".  
Medusa the nurse sells him on MAID, because it would make him sleep well.  
The wife doesn't know it but the clinic is behind on its organ quota and needs more fresh organs. They intend to take her organs next.  
Medusa needs to do several more MAID procedures in order to get a down payment on a house in Quebec.  
Steve is trafficking humans for his innovative black market operation in organ harvesting for quick cash.  
The curtain closes for act 2 and an intermission is called.  

## Influences
The clinic is inside a high-tech hospital in downtown Vancouver, where the rents are skyrocketing and more bodies need to be processed to meet Steve's mortgage payments.  
This scene is heavily inspired by Sweeney Todd or "The Rocky Horror Show".  

## Easter Eggs
Don't worry, I did this 300 times and no one cried.: This is a reference to doctor death who was convicted of serial murder in the UK.
