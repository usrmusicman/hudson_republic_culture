# Summary

## Story
This piece is about a plant known as Justin Trudeau and his friend Steve.  
Steve found him at a China shop ran by a strange man known only as Mark.  
Justin told Steve to feed him more, he always needs more.  
Steve concocts an amazing scheme to grow the budget, he creates a green slush known as "the fund".  
A female back bench staffer finds out and does not know what to do, but in good conscious she resigns from the Liberal party.  

## Influences
This musical number is based on "Little Shop of Horrors" act 1 opening theme.  
"Let It Grow" has that Disney influence from the film "Let It Go".  

## Easter Eggs
Is that a plant from the China shop?: Canada is enticed by the magic of China and all it has to offer.  
The government instructions say "feed me"!: This is the Westminster monster consuming everything in sight, this hungry beast is known as Justin Trudeau. Also, it is a "Little Shop of Horrors" musical number.  
TOTAL ECLIPSE OF THE SUN!!...: This is referencing the April 28, 2024 (10 years after this scene) total solar eclipse over Ontario and this is the foreshadowing event for Canada's end.
