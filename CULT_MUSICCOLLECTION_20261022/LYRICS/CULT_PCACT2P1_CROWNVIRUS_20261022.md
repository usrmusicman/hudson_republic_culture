# Summary

## Story
The countryside family from act 1 is sitting in a 1950s style diner burger joint and the daughter wants to go see the beaver animatronic.  
The father puts a 25 cent coin into the jukebox machine and mentions "This box is cheaper than the TTC subway fare 😂!" Then, the music starts to play.  
He goes to exchange his 5 dollar bill and the cash register freaks out, printing out 100s of dollars.  
When the "Bite of 1867" is mentioned the beaver animatronic starts twitching and making sounds.  

## Influences
This is a 1950s themed set design for the diner.  
This scene is inspired by the Five Nights At Freddie's game franchise.  

## Easter Eggs
I got part two of the bug today! I thought they squashed it back in 2003?: This is a throwback to the first SARS outbreak back in 2003.  
The crown-virus is no threat!: COVID-19 (Corona-virus), Corona literally means crown and the Westminster virus is seen as terminal.  
All I see is fighting for the toilet-trees.: This one is making fun of the Hysteria in Costco and other big box stores over the lack of TP. Also, this showed Canadians had little disaster preparedness, something the prepping community warned for years.  
Is today the fifth night?... Is that the bite of 1867?... Did I just see an animatronic beaver doing the tail slap?...: This is a play on the FNAF tropes and the Bite of '87 mentioned by YouTuber Markiplier. The bite of '87 was adapted to bite of 1867.
