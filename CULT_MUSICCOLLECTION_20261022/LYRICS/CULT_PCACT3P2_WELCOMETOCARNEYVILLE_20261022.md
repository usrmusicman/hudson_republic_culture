# Summary

## Story
The stage is dark and then we here a nursery rhyme, after which the stage lights turn on again.  
Peggy is then placed in the circle tied down and is lectured from the book "values".  
Steve is hungry for the sacrifice and Carney starts fueling up the chainsaw, ready to take her innocent soul off this Earth.  
She screams as they cut one of her leg irons.  
The scene dims at the end.  

## Influences
This is a horrifying circus show inside a dark dank Carnival test.  
Mark is a crazed clown out for blood and will stop at nothing to use any trick, including illusions to trick his subjects into submission.  

## Easter Eggs
Ring around the Carney... A pocket full of patsies... Ashes, ashes... we all fall down...: This is the twisted technocrat version of the children's nursery rhyme "Ring Around The Rosy".  
Now enters a man who is slender... contracted to bend her...: This is a reference to the Slenderman games and the technocrat Mark Carney is Canada's slender man.  
Now Carney is here... and the nine pages are near...: This is a reference to the fan game called Slender (The Nine Pages) and the 9 pages climate agenda approved by the Liberal Party of Canada.
