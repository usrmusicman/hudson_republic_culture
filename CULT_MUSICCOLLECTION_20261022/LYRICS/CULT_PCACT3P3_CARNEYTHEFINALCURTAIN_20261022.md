# Summary

## Story
Peggy is about to be sliced open and sacrificed by Mark Carney, when Brad the Beaver appears, this is before Mark Carney can have his way.  
Brad battles Mark Carney to the death and saves Peggy and the rest of the innocent Canadians.  
Brad then leaves to go get his pet elephant called Jumbo and other beaver friends to take down the Maple Mafia once and for all.  

## Influences
This is the epic battle with Mark Carney and the salvation of Peggy.  

## Easter Eggs
