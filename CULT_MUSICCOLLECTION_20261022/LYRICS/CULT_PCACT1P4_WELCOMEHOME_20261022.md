# Summary

## Story
The father and family that left Toronto get a warm greeting from local country town folk.  
They arrive into such a welcoming environment and here just the sounds of barn animals and farm activities.  
Curtain drawn for the end of Act 1.  

## Influences
The staging is heavily inspired by the hit film "Legends of the Fall".  
Also, the atmosphere is like the opening of "Old Yeller".  

## Easter Eggs
The wheat and rye will smack, while the Horseshoe becomes Iraq! Now come and meet my Uncle Sam, at least he ain't from Afghanistan!: Some people reference the golden horseshoe as a middle east caliphate.  
Now always move around them toes, don't listen to the paid-off hoes!: A knock against the 4th wave feminists and the feminine tampon government. HOES, Huron, Ontario, Erie, Superior (Canada's great lakes).  
They think that we are hicks, but they can't survive in the sticks!: A Redneck Rampage (1997) reference.  
We are healthy as a horse, at least Monsanto ain't our main course!: This makes fun of the GMO seeds, chemicals in the water and Monsanto's control of the Agriculture Industrial Complex.  
We spend all day in the sun, we can't let Ottawa take our guns!: Mentions the on going gun grabs by the Federal Liberals.  
I wonder what is about to... come!...: Foreshadowing for act 2.  
I wear a badge full of merit.: This is paying homage to the Beaver scouts, the genesis image of the Hudson Republic. Merit Badges should be earned and fought for.
