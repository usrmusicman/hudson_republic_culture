# Summary

## Story
The cabinet ministers in parliament giggle and joke about wearing each others suits and shoes (ballet shoes) before session.  
A lyrical soprano in a red dress and black heels enters the room and tells the story lyrics in detail to the audience.  
They all dance and prance like ballerinas around the cabinet floor for the Chorus and Outro lines.  
Mark Carney enters the scene and is depicted as a reptilian type character.  
People are using opera glasses from the balconies to see the political circus below.  

## Influences
Dark opera comedy type scene, with a dance number similar to that of "Verdi's Brindisi from La Traviata".  
The spoken word section is like a child's bedtime story.  
The politicians love to cross the floor, against the will of the people.  

## Easter Eggs
There once was a mouse, in the legislative house. Then, they brought in the Waffen infantry!: This was when the Liberals brought in a Waffen SS veteran into parliament without vetting him.  
Then came Chrystia, who flew to Guyana. She held a gold visa, she knew how to treat a queen!: Compares the Liberals to a Jonestown cult.  
Well there once was mass inside the CAF, but now it's a recruitment tool for insurgency!: This blames policy around allowing mass-importation of foreigners into the Canadian military.  
Are you sure it's not a sleeping Hamas? They're not patriots so give them a pass!: This is a direct shot from the Liberals to our veterans. They don't give a damn.  
Blue dot fever is in and no one will go!: No one wants to see Justin Trudeau, like they don't want to pay for luxury priced rock concerts. Also, blue is the Conservative color in Canada.  
Well in this house is a conman's blouse. It's an oversized scarf written in Trudeau's barf!: This makes fun of Trudeau being the tampon gender.  
They are rooking the guests and they're cooking the books!: This is a direct reference to a Les Miserables line from Master of the House. "They are rooking the guests and they're cooking the books".
